# OPSD arms A-D — results, 2026-09-01

Repo `~/claudecode/opsd`, branch `opsd-arms` (cut from `contrastive-opsd`).
Prior: HANDOFF_2026-08-27, -08-30. Data: 3,726 pair-problems (3,353 train /
373 held-out), hard Countdown n∈{5,6}, honest grader throughout.

## HEADLINE (updated after the STaR control was fixed)

**Plain self-training BEATS contrastive OPSD by ~12 points on IDENTICAL
data.** SFT on the model's own correct FULL-REASONING traces gains +2.9
(data-matched, CI [+0.9,+4.8]) or +4.1 (498-example version); every OPSD arm
loses 9-10. STaR needs ~50 min of GPU vs 9 h per arm.

The original arm D reported 0.071 and was VOID: it was SFT'd on
think-stripped x_plus, so it learned to answer without reasoning (1,182
tokens vs base 9,500). Sanjeev caught this. The broken control made OPSD
look merely disappointing rather than clearly worse than the trivial
alternative.

All three planned contrasts are NULL:
* **A − B = −0.015** [−0.037, +0.006] — the grader-RL teacher does not help
  downstream, despite better grading (0.893 vs 0.732 pair-accuracy), better
  slot steering (−0.63→−0.28) and transfer to standalone verification.
* **A − C = −0.016** [−0.036, +0.005] — loss placement does not matter.
  Concentrating on the answer region ≈ uniform. The measured 100:1 dilution
  does not convert into a training benefit.
* **C − B = +0.000** — arms mutually indistinguishable.

## Secondary finding: length collapse ≠ the damage mechanism

Arm-1's story was that OPSD damage shows up as length collapse. Here the
two DISSOCIATE:
* Teacher determines length direction: base teacher → −22% (7443), RL'd
  teacher → +6% (10069/10071). Reproduced in training curves and at eval.
* Accuracy damage is the same either way (−9.0 vs −10.5), and the arm that
  held length longest (A) lost the MOST pass@8 (−15.3 vs B's −7.0).
So length is a teacher-transmission effect, not the injury.

## Caveats (must appear in any writeup)

1. Single seed per arm; A/B/C differences (~1.5 pts) are within noise, only
   the base-vs-arm gap is robust.
2. Signal scale: 53 steps, 1 epoch, 3,353 problems (Arm 1 was 91 steps).
3. Arm D (think-stripped) is VOID — replaced by D2/D3. D3 is the DATA-MATCHED
   control: all 3,353 train problems, k=4 fresh thinking rollouts (13,412
   generated, 6,661 correct+complete), one trace per problem, 16k SFT window
   (only 3 of 2,707 dropped). D2 (+4.1) and D3 (+2.9) agree within noise, so
   the self-training gain is real and saturates ~+3 to +4; it does not grow
   with 5x the data at 3 epochs.
4. Damage onset is ABRUPT, not cumulative: gate initial-live-fraction sits
   at ~0.35 for 5 steps then roughly doubles to ~0.70 within 1-2 steps of
   the LR reaching full 5e-6 (warmup ratio 0.03 ≈ 1.6 steps), and plateaus
   for the remaining 47. Not a truncation artifact (arm A's outputs got
   LONGER, which would push live-fraction down). Implies the first
   full-strength gradients do the injury -> lr is the first knob to vary
   (5e-7 / 1e-6 arm) to separate "wrong method" from "wrong dose".

## What this means

The diagnosis chain built 08-29/30 (whole-trace forward-pass margin ≈ 0;
signal concentrated at the answer slot +0.84 nats and mid-trace slips +1.39)
correctly described the teacher's conditionals, and the grader-RL did move
them. But neither improving the teacher nor placing the loss where the
signal lives rescues the method: contrastive OPSD reduces solve rate on this
task under every configuration tried. Either the sparse signal is too small
relative to whatever the distillation objective costs, or the damage
mechanism is something the margin measurements do not capture.
