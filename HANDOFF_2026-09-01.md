# OPSD arms A-D — results, 2026-09-01

Repo `~/claudecode/opsd`, branch `opsd-arms` (cut from `contrastive-opsd`).
Prior: HANDOFF_2026-08-27, -08-30. Data: 3,726 pair-problems (3,353 train /

## HEADLINE (updated after the STaR control was fixed)

**Plain self-training BEATS contrastive OPSD by ~12 points on IDENTICAL
data.** SFT on the model's own correct FULL-REASONING traces gains +2.9
(data-matched, CI [+0.9,+4.8]) or +4.1 (498-example version); every OPSD arm
loses 9-10. STaR needs ~50 min of GPU vs 9 h per arm.

The original arm D reported 0.071 and was VOID: it was SFT'd on
think-stripped x_plus, so it learned to answer without reasoning (1,182
tokens vs base 9,500). Sanjeev caught this. The broken control made OPSD
look merely disappointing rather than clearly worse than the trivial
alternative.

## Secondary finding: length collapse ≠ the damage mechanism

Arm-1's story was that OPSD damage shows up as length collapse. Here the
two DISSOCIATE:
* Teacher determines length direction: base teacher → −22% (7443), RL'd
  teacher → +6% (10069/10071). Reproduced in training curves and at eval.
* Accuracy damage is the same either way (−9.0 vs −10.5), and the arm that
  held length longest (A) lost the MOST pass@8 (−15.3 vs B's −7.0).
So length is a teacher-transmission effect, not the injury.

## Caveats (must appear in any writeup)

1. Single seed per arm; A/B/C differences (~1.5 pts) are within noise, only
   the base-vs-arm gap is robust.
2. Signal scale: 53 steps, 1 epoch, 3,353 problems (Arm 1 was 91 steps).
3. Arm D (think-stripped) is VOID — replaced by D2/D3. D3 is the DATA-MATCHED
   control: all 3,353 train problems, k=4 fresh thinking rollouts (13,412
   generated, 6,661 correct+complete), one trace per problem, 16k SFT window
   (only 3 of 2,707 dropped). D2 (+4.1) and D3 (+2.9) agree within noise, so
   the self-training gain is real and saturates ~+3 to +4; it does not grow
   with 5x the data at 3 epochs.
4. Damage onset is ABRUPT, not cumulative: gate initial-live-fraction sits
   at ~0.35 for 5 steps then roughly doubles to ~0.70 within 1-2 steps of
   the LR reaching full 5e-6 (warmup ratio 0.03 ≈ 1.6 steps), and plateaus
   for the remaining 47. Not a truncation artifact (arm A's outputs got
   LONGER, which would push live-fraction down). Implies the first
   full-strength gradients do the injury -> lr is the first knob to vary
   (5e-7 / 1e-6 arm) to separate "wrong method" from "wrong dose".

## Sign checks: BOTH correct (2026-09-03)

Sanjeev asked whether the loss sign is right on wrong rollouts. Checked in
code and by measurement:

* **Loss sign correct.** `_jsd_per_token` (trainer.py:1939) is a proper
  symmetric JSD (alpha=0.5), >= 0, minimized -> student pulled TOWARD the
  teacher on its own wrong rollouts. Standard on-policy distillation. The
  only clipped ratio anywhere is the vLLM engine-mismatch IS weight
  (one-sided cap 2.0, per-sequence multiplier); no policy-ratio clipping
  exists because JSD is not a policy gradient. In the grader-RL, verl's PPO
  clip is present but inert (clipfrac 0.0 at every step, on-policy).
* **Effect sign also correct** (`rl/eval_teacher_on_wrong.py`, n=200 wrong
  traces): mean per-token logprob of the wrong trace under bare base
  −0.4022; base+pair −0.4885 (delta −0.086, −56 sigma); RL'd+pair −0.5588
  (delta −0.157, −69 sigma). **0% positive in both cases.** The teacher
  dispreferres wrong traces, and the RL'd teacher dispreferres them ~2x
  more. The hypothesis that x- in context makes the teacher wrong-preferring
  is REFUTED.

So: teacher grades well, dislikes wrong traces when scoring, dislikes them
more after RL -- and distilling it still costs 9-10 points. The damage
mechanism is NOT identified. Ruled out: loss sign, effect sign,
wrong-preferring teacher, grading incompetence, loss placement, dilution.

Unestablished hypothesis worth one arm: **privileged-context mismatch** --
the teacher's conditionals are those of a model that has read two worked
attempts; the student never has that context, so it is trained toward a
distribution it cannot justify from its own inputs. Weak supporting hint:
the RL'd teacher is 1.8x further from the unconditioned model and arm A was
worst (−10.5), but arm C shares that teacher and scored −9.0, and A/B/C are
mutually indistinguishable -- so this is suggestive only. Direct test:
distill with the teacher seeing NO pair (pure self-distillation on wrong
rollouts). If that damages too, the injury is in on-policy wrong-rollout
distillation itself, not the privileged context.

## Failure audit + low-LR arm (2026-09-04)

**Low-LR arm E** (= arm B at lr 5e-7 instead of 5e-6): −5.9 [−8.0, −3.8].
Damage is dose-DEPENDENT but does not vanish; 10x smaller steps buy back
only a third, and the length collapse persists (−18% vs −22%). Implies the
gradient DIRECTION is harmful and smaller steps merely travel less far
along it — not a tuning problem. Damage trajectory (arm B checkpoints):
step15 −7.4, step30 −8.4, step52 −9.0, i.e. 82% done by step 15 of 52.

**Failure audit** (`rl/eval_failure_audit.py`, 100 gens each, hand-verified):

| | correct | arith wrong | multiset viol | truncated | no box |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | 51% | 8% | 30% | 8% | 3% |
| armB | 43% | 14% | **43%** | 0% | 0% |

* NOT a grading artifact, and the bias runs the SAFE way: base loses 11%
  of generations to format failures (cap-truncation, no box), arm B loses
  0%. Correcting for format would WIDEN the gap.
* Six sampled failures re-derived by hand — all correctly graded.
* Damage signature is specific: constraint (multiset) violations 30%→43%.
  The dominant failure in both models is hitting the target then padding
  with SELF-CANCELLING terms that reuse numbers: `+(83-83)`,
  `x(7-1)/(7-1)`, `x((15-11-24)/(15-11-24))`. OPSD makes the student reach
  for this hack ~43% more often.

**Contamination/priming hypothesis: REFUTED** (`rl/eval_cheat_span.py`,
n=150, logprob of the cheat span itself). The teacher DISCOURAGES the
cheat: delta vs base-no-pair −0.070 (arm B teacher, −3.3σ) and −0.154
(RL'd teacher, −4.0σ). And the effect is BACKWARDS from the priming story:
when the pair's own x- is itself a cheat the teacher discourages MORE
(−0.076 / −0.171) than when it is clean (−0.041 / −0.074). Sanjeev's
original intuition — the teacher should give the cheat low probability —
is correct.

**So the paradox is sharp**: teacher grades well, dislikes wrong traces,
specifically dislikes the cheat, and training toward it makes the student
cheat 43% more.

**Best remaining explanation: SIGNAL-TO-NOISE, not sign.** The corrective
component is ~0.07-0.15 nats on ~10 cheat tokens (and ~0 whole-trace
margin); the imitative component spans ~10k tokens of the student's own
wrong reasoning that the loss asks it to match distributionally. Training
predominantly teaches "reproduce the neighborhood of your own failures".
Consistent with: damage in ~3 steps (immediate drift, not an outpaced
corrective process), dose-dependence without benefit, STaR winning by
training on 2,704 CORRECT traces instead, and the inverse pattern that arm
A's teacher discourages the cheat MOST (−0.154) while arm A scored WORST
(−10.5).

**LAST UNTESTED CONFIGURATION — span-only weighting.** Loss on the ~25
boxed answer tokens only (w_mid=0, eps elsewhere): the +0.84-nat slot
signal becomes nearly all of the gradient and the 10k wrong-reasoning
tokens contribute ~nothing. Arms A/B used tail-window (last 4k, still
mostly wrong prose); C used uniform. If the SNR story holds, this is the
only variant that could come out non-negative. One 13h job; the patch
already implements the mode.

## What this means

The diagnosis chain built 08-29/30 (whole-trace forward-pass margin ≈ 0;
signal concentrated at the answer slot +0.84 nats and mid-trace slips +1.39)
correctly described the teacher's conditionals, and the grader-RL did move
them. But neither improving the teacher nor placing the loss where the
signal lives rescues the method: contrastive OPSD reduces solve rate on this
task under every configuration tried. Either the sparse signal is too small
relative to whatever the distillation objective costs, or the damage
mechanism is something the margin measurements do not capture.
