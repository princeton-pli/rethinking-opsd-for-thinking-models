## Results (all on 92 held-out pairs / same-300-problem probes)

* **RL effect: null by pre-registration.** Pair-margin +0.0001 nats; probe
  cells within noise. Weight-level: ΔW absmax ~2e-5 (below bf16 ULP) — the
  dose was homeopathic (lr 1e-6 × 40 steps), pipeline itself validated.
* **HEADLINE (base-model property): the forward-pass margin OPSD distills
  from is ~zero.** Whole-trace mean per-token margin of x⁺ vs x⁻ under
  pair context: −0.0018 nats, 46.7% positive — no correctness signal,
  and WORSE than bare context (+0.0199). Same model adjudicates these
  pairs generatively at 65–81%. The §3 insight, now measured in OPSD's
  exact regime on the task where generation-side adjudication works.
* **Answer-token margin refines it**: +0.648 nats mean but only 52.2%
  positive (heavy right tail — a minority of pairs carries it); bare
  context +0.603, so the pair adds only ~0.05. Correctness signal exists
  in the conditionals but is (a) concentrated on answer tokens (~1500x
  dilution in a uniform distillation loss) and (b) mostly the model's own
  solving prior, not pair-reading.
* Transfer cells (Sanjeev's grading→solving hypothesis): none/think
  0.422→0.458, none/nothink 0.033→0.043 — right sign, ~1.3σ, not evidence
  at this dose.

## LATE ADDITION (2026-08-30 ~01:00) — the sparse-signal result

Sanjeev's hypothesis (near-zero trace-mean is compatible with concentrated
learning-relevant signal) tested directly on wrong rollouts
(`rl/eval_sparse_signal.py`, results `rl/sparse_signal_base.jsonl`):

* **Answer-slot steering: pair-specific increment +0.925 nats, 29σ,
  positive on 90.9% of 922 wrong rollouts.** At the wrong trace's own
  \boxed{ slot, pair context moves the teacher toward the correct
  expression by ~a nat (bare −1.43 → pair −0.50). The contrastive signal
  is LARGE exactly where the outcome-gated loss acts.
* **KL localization**: the pair perturbs conditionals broadly
  (0.4–0.9 nats/token, early-mid-trace prose heavy; math tokens carry
  20.2% of KL mass on 42.3% of tokens) — uniform distillation spends ~99%
  of gradient on pair-conditioned STYLE, burying the slot signal ~100:1.
* Reconciliation of all margin measurements: whole-trace ~0; clean-
  continuation answer tokens +0.65 mostly solving-prior (pair +0.05);
  wrong-trace answer slot pair-specific +0.92 — decisive where the model
  cannot solve alone.

**Design implication (now measured, not speculative): token-weighted
contrastive-OPSD** — distill on wrong rollouts with loss weight
concentrated on the answer region — has a 29σ mechanism. Grader-RL's
target likewise sharpens to amplifying the slot increment.
